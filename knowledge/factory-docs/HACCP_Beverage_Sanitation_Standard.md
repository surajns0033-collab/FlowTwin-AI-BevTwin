# HACCP & Sanitary Beverage Quality Standard (BevTwin Operations)

## Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points (HACCP)
1. **CCP-1: Pasteurization Heat Lethality (M05 Tubular Flash Pasteurizer)**
   - Minimum temperature: 72.5°C with 15-second retention tube dwell time.
   - Pasteurization Units (PU): Target 15.0 – 25.0 PU for fruit juices and organic iced teas.
   - Continuous flow divert valve (FDV) auto-trips if core product temp drops below 71.8°C.

2. **CCP-2: Dissolved Oxygen (DO) & Carbonation Equilibrium (M02 / M04 / M07)**
   - Vacuum Deaeration limit: Dissolved O2 must remain < 15 ppb prior to filling.
   - Carbonation volumes: 3.5 – 4.2 vol CO2 for sparkling seltzers; 1.8 – 2.4 vol CO2 for kombucha.
   - Headspace liquid nitrogen (LN2) dosing at M08 ensures < 0.8% residual oxygen in sealed cans.

3. **CCP-3: Can Seam Integrity & Fill-Height Inspection (QC01 / QC02 / M08)**
   - Seam double-fold thickness: 1.15mm ± 0.05mm.
   - Seam overlap: > 1.10mm (acoustic pulse verification at QC02).
   - High-speed vision + gamma fill rejection for underfills exceeding 2.5ml.
